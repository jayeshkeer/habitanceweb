# Habitance OS — copy and launch checklist

This is a **design preview only**. Imagine/core selectors switch local mock content. The page does not send or store visitor details, society records, AI requests, bank actions, government filings, WhatsApp messages or emails. The lead-intake endpoint remains gated.

| Placement | Replace or verify before launch |
| --- | --- |
| Audience | Confirm the target is housing societies serving 100+ members. The form asks separately for society-unit count. |
| Reality Check | Confirm that manager follow-up, a Sunday managing committee meeting, WhatsApp complaints, government notices and legal notices reflect your audience and preferred terms. |
| Records preview | Confirm the applicable files, registers, minutes, registrar filings and flat-transfer forms for your region. The current copy does not promise continuous updates or one-click retrieval. Demonstrate those capabilities before adding such promises. |
| Daily digest | The page calls this a proposed AI-assisted summary of reports, decisions and suggested follow-ups; it does not promise automated assignments. Verify the data sources, accuracy and human review. The earlier one-minute reading target is omitted; add it only after measurement. |
| Fixed deposits and local filings | Confirm maturity dates, maintenance-contract renewals, applicable state filings and committee approvals. No bank connection or automatic renewal is claimed or connected. Have a qualified professional review the applicable MCS Act and other state/local requirements. |
| Tenant and vendor approvals | Define who reviews tenant no-objection certificates, vendor contracts and member concerns. The page shows review steps as a concept; do not add claims of pre-vetting or automated triage unless proven. |
| Society forms and registers | Confirm whether Form I / J applies, and verify the correct forms, records, terminology and current process with a qualified local professional. The page uses samples, not live government records or filings. |
| Five experience previews | Verify the scope and behavior of Daily System Report, Decision Engine, society forms/registers, notice follow-up and permissioned member records before describing them as available features. Current selectors are illustrative only. |
| Member experience | Confirm which records and minutes members may view, how access is determined, who reviews concerns and what evidence supports lower paperwork or clearer governance. No live permission settings or member data are connected. |
| Member-record mock view | Replace bracketed date/excerpt/access fields only with approved sample content. Never display private society records or imply live access control. |
| Society logos | Replace placeholders only with logos that named societies permit you to use. |
| Testimonials | Replace placeholders with approved, verifiable quotes and verified speaker identities/roles, or remove them. Do not invent outcomes or adoption numbers. |
| Walkthrough preview | The five-field layout is illustrative; no details are accepted. After design approval, confirm fields, processing behavior, data-use wording, notification delivery and thank-you state. Only label it as a booking after booking is actually enabled. |
| Email notifications | No provider or recipient settings are active. In the functional phase, provide the provider key, destination inbox and verified sender through the protected settings card. |
| WhatsApp affordance | No live chat number is connected. Provide the approved business WhatsApp number with country code before enabling a link. |
| Claims removed from the page | “100% compliant,” “Zero Legal Exposure,” “Zero Penalties,” “Maximum Yields,” and the one-minute/60-second read-time claim are not used in the current copy. Reintroduce only with credible evidence and qualified legal/financial review; otherwise keep them out. |
| Domain and social metadata | After a production domain is approved, configure canonical and absolute Open Graph URLs and recheck social previews. |

All editable page copy and visible mock-preview labels are centralized in `client/src/content/siteCopy.ts`.
